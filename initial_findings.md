# Initial Findings — Marketing & Sales Analytics

> **Data note:** This project uses a synthetic practice dataset created for learning. These figures do not represent a real company or real market performance.

## Dataset snapshot
- Transactions: 30
- Units sold: 86
- Total recorded revenue: ₹48,341.25
- Average revenue per transaction: ₹1,611.38
- Date range: 2026-01-03 to 2026-03-02

## Descriptive findings
- **Top revenue region:** North (₹15,170.70, 31.4% of sample revenue).
- **Top sales channel by revenue:** Online (₹32,604.85, 67.4% of sample revenue).
- **Top product by revenue:** USB-C Hub (₹15,814.45).
- **Top category by revenue:** Electronics (₹23,444.90).

## Managerial implications to investigate
1. Examine whether the North region's higher sample revenue reflects more orders, larger baskets, or product mix.
2. Compare online and store average order values and customer repeat behaviour before reallocating channel budgets.
3. Investigate the USB-C Hub's revenue contribution, availability, and margin before prioritising promotions.
4. Review regional and category performance using a larger, time-balanced dataset before making investment decisions.

## Limitations
- Only 30 synthetic transactions are included.
- The sample covers an incomplete March period and is not suitable for seasonality claims.
- Costs, profit margins, marketing spend, campaign exposure, and customer demographics are not provided.
- Revenue is calculated as quantity × unit price × (1 − discount rate); it is not profit.

## Next steps
- Add a larger dataset with consistent time coverage.
- Create product, region, and channel charts.
- Add margin and marketing-spend data if available.
- Validate insights before turning them into business recommendations.
